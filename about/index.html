I'm L-RiXx — an independent-minded developer and digital explorer interested in understanding how things work, building useful tools, and turning ideas into working projects.

01 / Who I am

I'm drawn to the intersection of software, systems, and experimentation. I enjoy going beyond using technology to exploring how it works and what can be built with it.

My interests range from application development and programming languages to Linux environments, and game development. Each area offers a different way to solve problems and understand the digital world.

I don't see every project as something that must immediately become a finished product. Sometimes the goal is to learn a concept, test an idea, or discover a better way to approach a problem.

04 / How I approach things

Understand, experiment, build, improve.

I prefer learning by doing: breaking a problem into smaller parts, investigating how those parts work, testing possible solutions, and refining the result.

Progress doesn't always mean writing more code. Sometimes it means finding a simpler design, understanding a limitation, or deciding that an idea needs a different approach.
